Spotifai is an ad-free Spotify client for Android, with lossless downloads and audio extensions, on a free account.

Spotifai is a rebranded fork of Myspoti (https://github.com/kota-zo/myspoti), released under GPL-3.0.

Features:

- Spotify with no ads
- Lossless downloads, encrypted and kept on your phone
- Audio and download-source extensions you add yourself
- Background playback and Spotify Connect
- Synced lyrics, with translation
- Local files and your own library
- Playlist import and export
- Equalizer, playback speed and skip silence
- Sleep timer
- Light, dark and AMOLED themes

Spotifai is a new fork and has no installs or reviews yet — be the first.

This is an independent third-party client. Not affiliated with Spotify AB.
